Cutter Radius Compensation Limitations within Inside Corners

For an inside corner, the controller does not generate a short link arc. This causes a small piece of material that does not get cut by the cutting tool to stay in the inside corner. The following figure shows this material.

Figure: Material Not Cut

The following figure shows that the radius of the cutting tool is too large for the shape of the part. This type of overcutting can occur in the inside corners and causes the controller to generate a task warning. You can continue to operate with the conditions that cause this warning.

Figure: Radius of the Cutting Tool is Too Large

The following figure shows that the radius of the cutting tool is too large for the curve. This type of overcutting can occur in the inside corners and causes the controller to generate a task warning. You can continue to operate with the conditions that cause this warning.

Figure: Programmed Arc Smaller than Cutting Tool
