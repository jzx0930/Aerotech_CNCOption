G16 Command (Coordinate System #1 Axis Assignment)

Syntax: 
G16 <axisMask>

Example:

 
G16 u v w

Use G16 to simultaneously assign values to I, J, and K. I is assigned to the first axis in the mask, J to the second axis, and K to the third axis. I, J, and K are used only with G2 and G3 commands. With G17, G18, and G19, they define the plane in which the G2/G3 circular motion is generated.

Note: The order of the axes in the axis mask is significant because it determines the mapping of the I, J, K. For example, G16 X Y Z is different than G16 Z Y X.

By default:

  • I is 0 (X axis)
  • J is 1 (Y axis)
  • K is 2 (Z axis)
