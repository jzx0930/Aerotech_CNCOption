Plane Selection for Coordinate System 2 Commands

Syntax: 
G27
G28 
G29

G27, G28, and G29 are equivalent to G17, G18, and G19, respectively, but they select the plane for coordinate system 2 instead of coordinate system 1. The G27, G28 and G29 commands are used in conjunction with G26, G12, and G13 to produce circular motion on coordinate system 2. By default, the plane selected by G27 is active.

The following figure illustrates the use of these commands. In the figure G27 selects Plane 1, G28 selects Plane 2, and G29 selects Plane 3. The active plane is indicated by Bits 26 to 28 of the TaskStatus2 status item.
